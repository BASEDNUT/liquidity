Orchard Atlas — instrument surface

The Orchard models Based Nut liquidity as a rooted, multiplex hypergraph: NUT root asset → wNUT rootstock → Branches, Grafts and Whorls → Groves → the whole evolving state machine.

Dataset is a static, honestly-labeled snapshot — swap orchard-data.js for a live endpoint when one exists. Nothing here is simulated; every number traces to the reconciled cache or on-chain verification.
