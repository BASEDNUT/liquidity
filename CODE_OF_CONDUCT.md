# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are the Nut Army. We keep the orchard welcoming for all nuts — peanuts, almonds, cashews, pecans, hazelnuts, coconuts, and chestnuts alike.

## Standards

- Be nutty but respectful
- No harassment, hate, or personal attacks
- Disagree on tech, not on people
- Keep memes funny, not cruel
- Help newcomers crack into the ecosystem

## Enforcement

Violations get reported to the community moderators on Twitter/X (@BASEDNUT_). Repeat offenders get sent to the dead address.

## Scope

Applies to all BASED NUT community spaces: GitHub, Twitter, Discord/Telegram, and the Liquidity Portal.
